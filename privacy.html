Privacy Policy

Neuromancer is a personal assistant app used only by Jordan Conley. It is not offered to the public.

With Jordan's permission, it accesses his own Google account (Gmail, Calendar and Drive) to help him read, organize and manage his own email, calendar and files. That data is processed on his own computer and is not sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising or any other purpose.

Access can be revoked at any time from the Google account's security settings.
